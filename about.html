Our story

Twenty years, one household at a time.

Mishah Employment Agency began in 2004 as a small agency helping a handful of local households find reliable domestic help. There was no advertising budget behind it — just one family recommending us to another, and one careful placement leading to the next. That's still how most of our work comes to us today.

We're one of the earlier employment agencies to be formally registered with Brunei's Department of Labour, and we've held that licence continuously ever since — through every renewal, every change in regulation, every year in between. Two decades in, the approach hasn't changed: understand what a household actually needs, understand what a helper actually wants, and take the time to do the paperwork properly rather than rush a match that isn't right for either side.

What we hold to

The principles behind every placement.

01

Proper documentation

Every placement follows the paperwork and permit requirements set out under Brunei labour regulations — no shortcuts.

02

Honest matching

We tell employers when a candidate isn't a fit, and tell candidates when a role isn't right for them, rather than force a placement.

03

Staying reachable

Our relationship with an employer or employee doesn't end at placement — we remain a point of contact afterwards.
