Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This explains what personal information Mishah Employment Agency collects, why, and how it's protected.

This policy is a general template describing how this website and Mishah Employment Agency intend to handle personal information. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be checked against applicable Brunei Darussalam regulations before being relied on as a final legal document.

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on whether you're a family/employer or a domestic helper.

From employers: name, phone number, email, home address, household details relevant to the placement, and payment information for agency and package fees.

From domestic helpers: name, contact details, identity/passport information, work history and references, results of required medical examinations, and documents needed for work permit and visa processing.

How we use it

We use this information to match families with suitable helpers, verify eligibility and documentation, process work permits and visas, communicate with you about a placement, meet our obligations as a licensed employment agency, and improve how we operate.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it's necessary to arrange and support a placement:

  • Relevant Brunei government departments (e.g. Labour and immigration authorities) for permit, visa, and licensing requirements.
  • Medical examination and insurance providers involved in the placement process.
  • The employer or helper on the other side of a placement, to the extent needed to introduce and confirm a match.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

How long we keep it

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet record-keeping obligations as a licensed employment agency. Where you ask us to delete your information, we'll do so unless we're required to keep it for legal or regulatory reasons.

Keeping your information secure

We restrict access to personal information to staff who need it to do their work, and use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in between pages once you've created an account. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it, subject to any information we're required to retain for legal or regulatory reasons. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging or accepting domestic helper placements. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

Contact us
