Local helper, or amah from abroad?

The right process depends on who you're hiring. Here's what's involved either way, so you know what to expect before you start.

Requirements and timelines are set by Brunei's Department of Labour and Immigration Department and can change. This page is a general guide, not legal advice — we'll confirm the exact current requirements with you directly.

Local Domestic Helper

The simpler path.

If you hire a Bruneian domestic helper, there's no employer licence to apply for and no accommodation requirement — the process is mostly about finding the right person and agreeing clear terms.

Tell us what you need

Household size, duties, schedule (full-time or part-time), and start date.

We shortlist candidates

References and experience checked before anyone is put forward.

Interview & agree terms

You meet the candidate directly and agree working terms in writing.

Start & ongoing support

We stay reachable during the settling-in period.

Foreign Domestic Helper (Amah)

A more structured process.

Domestic helpers from abroad are commonly known in Brunei as amahs — most come from Indonesia or the Philippines, with Indonesian helpers often preferred by Malay households for shared language and faith. Hiring one involves a licensing and sponsorship framework overseen by Brunei's Department of Labour.

Licence

Employer licence required

You must hold a private licence from the Department of Labour before employing a foreign domestic helper.

Sponsorship

You're the sponsor

As sponsor, you're responsible for accommodation, food, healthcare, and a repatriation bond for the helper.

Timeline

Up to ~2 months

New recruitment from abroad typically takes around two months from application to arrival.

Two ways to hire

New recruitment, or a transfer maid.

New from abroad

We source and screen a candidate from overseas, then handle licensing, medical checks, and travel logistics. Takes longer, but you're choosing from a wider pool.

Transfer maid

Hire an amah already working in Brunei who's looking to change employers. Much faster, and you can interview her in person — but her identity card and pass need enough validity remaining to complete the transfer.

Initial consultation

We discuss your household's needs and whether new recruitment or a transfer maid fits better.

Licence application

We help you apply for the employer licence through the Department of Labour.

Medical & documentation

The helper completes required medical checks; we prepare the paperwork for immigration.

Work pass & entry

Immigration processes the work pass and, for new recruitment, arranges arrival.

Placement & ongoing support

We stay reachable for both you and the helper through the settling-in period and beyond.
