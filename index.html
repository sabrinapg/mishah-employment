How it works

A straightforward process, from first call to first day.

Tell us what you need

A family shares what kind of help they need, or a domestic helper shares their experience and availability — by phone, in person, or through our contact form.

We screen and shortlist

We verify documentation, check references, and match against the specific needs of the household or the helper's experience.

Interview & agreement

We arrange interviews, explain terms clearly to both sides, and prepare the paperwork and work permit documentation required under Brunei labour regulations.

Placement & follow-up

Once placed, we stay reachable for both the family and the helper during the settling-in period.
